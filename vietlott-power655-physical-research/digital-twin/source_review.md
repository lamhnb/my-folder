## Facts versus assumptions

55 balls, six-main/special roles and historical nominal ball size/mass anchor the research scope. All actual 2026 per-ball properties, motor speed/direction, motor/axis topology, bowl dimensions/depth, paddle shape, gate geometry, timing and loading state remain unknown. The baseline uses historical ball nominals only as a declared scenario. The 15–45 RPM sweep is an engineering assumption; **63.789 and 94.904 are never machine inputs**. Environment forces, aerodynamic drag beyond declared damping, foam deformation, electrostatics and vibration are unmodelled; no evidence available quantifies their influence for this hardware.

`parameter_registry.csv` distinguishes documented nominals, assumed scenario controls and unknown actual machine properties; ranges are sensitivity ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. Bullet contact properties are per-body coefficients; matching restitution/friction values on two bodies combine inside the engine and are not measured material constants. Rolling friction is Bullet's rolling-resistance parameter, not an experimentally calibrated rolling coefficient law. Distinct static/kinetic friction laws and contact stiffness are unknown.

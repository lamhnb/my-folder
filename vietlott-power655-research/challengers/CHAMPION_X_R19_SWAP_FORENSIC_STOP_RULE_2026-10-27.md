## Experiment 2: CrossRank score degeneracy
Each target's three numeric scores in this reconstruction are **identical**:
- stage 1 = 0.9722222222222222
- stage 2 = 0.891812865497076
- stage 3 = 0.8114035087719298

The source rank formula uses only positional indices and greedy selection, with three distinct noncycling swaps from original ranked sets. Consequently no target-varying score gap can distinguish favorable from unfavorable stage 3. Creating a threshold on these scores would only mimic a fixed stop count. This is a structural finding about rank-only score design, not a draw forecast edge.

## Experiment 3: strict-past trailing observed swap-return stop heuristic
Define for target t the past K evaluated target records, K=30,60,120,240. Compute each step's trailing average realized delta using **only prior targets**. Start at step 1; continue while step mean strictly positive; stop immediately before first non-positive step; if fewer than K evaluated past target records in this 624-record experiment, default to original 3 swaps. This sequential stop is computed before scoring the current target.

**Caution:** While each policy's selection decision uses preceding outcomes, all K and policy definitions have now been inspected on this same historical research window. They are not independent prospective evidence and cannot be promoted. The above simulation also begins its trailing history at row position 789; earlier actual results are not incorporated into the trailing policy. The stage-level deltas were generated by the frozen baseline's hypothetical full three-swap output, which is possible to compute from earlier results when simulating pretarget policies.

## Interpretation and safeguards
- Score thresholding is uninformative here because scores are invariant by stage.
- A hypothetical stop-at-two branch has a modest retrospective Selection advantage, but Selection >=5 count worsens per earlier swap-count ablation.
- Do not choose the policy with the largest historical retrospective total as Champion; future unseen draw results and independent Python verification remain necessary.
- Research should investigate pretarget **label-level input features** (momentum magnitude and margin, f30, f156, recent draw gaps, incoming/outgoing label ranks and provenance) against robust negative controls, with a prespecified holdout. This may expose predictable rules, but fair independent lottery draws should produce none.
- Never alter R19 Frozen or registered Champions. No promotion.
